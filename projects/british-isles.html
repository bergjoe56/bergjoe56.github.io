Thinking With A Pencil: British Isles

Thinking With A Pencil was a series of courses that I took while studying abroad. These courses included analysis of many historical sites, a studio focusing on urban parks, and a lecture series about the historical context and architectural theory of different periods. During my time I investigated urban environments, parks, and transportation hubs to research the impact of daily use, especially among those with disabilities and impairments. Through archival research, site analysis, and visual documentation, I also examined how buildings evolve, adapt, and continue to shape community life long after their initial construction. This study helped me develop a deeper awareness of context and the responsibility architects hold when intervening within existing environments. Together, these explorative works highlight my interest in architecture as both a practical and cultural discipline, one that is informed by people, history, and lived experience.

This series includes visual documentation of sites in and around the British Isles. Highlights include the British Museum, St Patrick’s Cathedral, Royal Botanic Garden, and Tottenham Hotspur Stadium.
